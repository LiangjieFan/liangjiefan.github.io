I am a PhD student at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool. My research lies at the intersection of Human–Computer Interaction (HCI), Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED), and inclusive digital innovation.

My work focuses on understanding how human-centered intelligent systems can enhance learning, support professional development, and promote digital inclusion across diverse populations.
