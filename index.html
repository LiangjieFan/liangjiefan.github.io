I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Liverpool, affiliated with Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. My research lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), Artificial Intelligence in Education (AIED), and inclusive digital innovation.

My work focuses on understanding how human-centered intelligent systems can enhance learning, support professional development, and promote digital inclusion across diverse populations.
