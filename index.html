About Me

I am a graduate researcher pursuing dual degrees at Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University and the University of Liverpool, with a strong background in educational technology and human-centered design. My work focuses on leveraging digital technologies to address real-world challenges and create inclusive, accessible solutions for diverse user groups.

Research Interests

My research interests are centered on Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), with particular emphasis on:

• Technology + Education (Tech+Edu)
Designing and evaluating technology-enhanced learning experiences that support diverse pedagogical contexts and learner needs.
• Technology + Ageing (Tech+Ageing)
Exploring accessible and user-friendly digital solutions that promote healthy ageing and intergenerational digital engagement.
• Technology + Diversity (Tech+Diversity)
Advancing inclusive design practices that ensure equitable access and meaningful participation across different demographic groups and abilities.
